## Benatar / 1979 gravel-rock
- PEOPLE 2025: striped shirt from first MTV video *You Better Run* still in her closet. Leotard + black tights + short boots + heavy liner was the 1977 Catch a Rising Star birth of the persona.
  https://people.com/pat-benatar-says-she-still-has-clothes-in-her-closet-from-the-1980s-exclusive-11753700
- NYT 2010: waist-cinched leotards, slick spandex tights, kohled eyes, peek-a-boo bangs, killer boots. No stylist.
  https://www.nytimes.com/2010/06/20/fashion/20town.html
- The List: red bodysuit + tights + statement black belt (1982); orange fringe top + black belt (Central Park 1980); black/white cropped shirt + high-waist pants + beret (1985).
  https://www.thelist.com/2178182/pat-benatar-iconic-80s-fashion-moments/
- Vintage News Daily photo set, late-70s through 80s stage clothes.
  https://vintagenewsdaily.com/40-fabulous-photos-show-fashion-styles-of-pat-benatar-in-the-late-1970s-and-during-the-80s/

East-TN translation: striped tank + high-waist jeans + thin leather headband + slouch ankle boots + W02 leather jacket. Not a costume-shop catsuit.

## 1600s / Golden Age privateer
- Gentlemen of Fortune: 1628–1706 slop contracts — kersey jacket, slop-breeches, Monmouth cap, double-soled shoes. Bucket boots are Hollywood, not shipboard.
  https://www.gentlemenoffortune.com/sailorskit.htm
- PiratesInfo Golden Age series: slop-breeches, shoes over boots, Anne Bonny/Mary Read in jackets + long trousers.
  https://www.piratesinfo.com/pirate-facts-and-pirate-legends/pirate-clothing-and-attire/pirate-attire-series-the-golden-age-1700-to-1740/
- World History Encyclopedia: canvas jackets and trousers; Bartholomew Roberts scarlet justacorps as captain flex; women in battle wore the men’s kit.
  https://www.worldhistory.org/article/1839/pirate-clothing-in-the-golden-age-of-piracy/
- Reconstructing History: women’s getting-dressed guide — stays, shift, petticoat vs the menswear disguise lane.
  https://reconstructinghistory.com/products/golden-age-of-piracy-womens-getting-dressed-guide

Heat privateer = lace stays + silk slops, not a velvet Halloween coat.

## Swahili / dhow / kanga
- Fair 2017 *A Decade of Design*: kanga composition locked 1876–1886 — border, centre field, Swahili cartouche.
- Prestholdt / textile-as-text: kanga/leso ~150x110 cm, worn in pairs; kitenge is the heavier tailored cousin.
- Fashioning Africa: freeborn Swahili women mark status with headcloth + ankle-length wrap; kanzu + kofia for men.
- Omiren: kanga vs kitenge — kanga speaks (text), kitenge is pattern-only tailored cloth.

True blouse = sleeved indigo tunic with kanga border, not a wrap-dress. Pair with W05 skirt + headwrap + sandals.

## Riverboat / 19c floating palace
- American Heritage *Heyday of the Floating Palace*: ladies’ cabin mirrors, bustle-era elegance, gingerbread 1860s–70s.
  https://www.americanheritage.com/heyday-floating-palace
- 1880s bustle stack (Meck presentation): combinations, long corset, lobster-tail bustle, petticoats, jacket-bodice.
- Dover / Norman photographs: real Natchez landing clothes vs hoopskirt myth.
- Gambling caricature c.1800 as the *myth* lane, not the work lane.

Rachel work riverboat = linen + leather cargo vest + canvas suspenders (W06). Heat riverboat = silk corset + slit bustle (H-RIVER).
